Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

The JK Espresso Tracker is run by TheJKExperience. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we only collect what the app needs to work, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we store your email address and first name. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password.
  • What you add to the app. Your brew logs, bean library, recipes, notes, ratings, and any photos you upload.
  • Your location, only if you allow it. The "find roasters near me" search on the Discover page asks your browser for your location. It is used only to run that search and is not saved to your account.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To save your brew logs, beans, recipes, and photos so you can see them on any device.
  • To send account emails, like the link to verify your email address.
  • To show coffee shops and roasters near you when you use the near me search.

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to show you ads. The app does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Services we rely on

A few outside services help run the app. Each only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase stores your account and everything you save in the app. Your data is private to your account, and other users cannot see it.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign in, and Gmail sends our account emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself.
  • OpenStreetMap powers the near me search. When you use it, your approximate location is sent to OpenStreetMap to find nearby shops.
  • jsDelivr and Google Fonts deliver some of the code and fonts the pages use.

Information saved on your device

Your browser keeps a small amount of information so you stay signed in and so the app can work when you add it to your home screen. You can clear this at any time by signing out or clearing your browser data.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. You can delete brews, beans, recipes, and photos in the app whenever you like. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email us at the address below and we will take care of it.

Keeping it safe

Your data is sent over secure connections and stored with access rules that limit it to your own account. No system is perfect, but we work to protect your information.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
